First public beta of Create: Kontor!

Found a company, license products, set prices, fulfill requests with your Create factories and grow your business in a fully simulated economy.

I have decided to release an public beta of Create: Kontor because I need feedback. It is quite hard to balance a large mod like that and I think that a public beta will help me in that process.

If you have any feedback, suggestions or problems, please let me know!

This is a beta: Things will change and worlds might break between versions. Please make frequent backups of your world.
